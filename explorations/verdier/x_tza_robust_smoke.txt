   211 . * **********************************************************************
   212 . * gen_vfirst: earliest non-missing value of `vname' per pid
   213 . * **********************************************************************
   214 . * Used by initial_values_robust / run_grc_robust to build the
   215 . * time-invariant cluster index v_i (first-wave province/region) that
   216 . * VV (2020) Section F requires for the robust extrapolation.
   217 . * Unit-tested at explorations/verdier/3_test_gen_vfirst.do.
   218 . * The buggy bysort pid: egen min(cond(!missing(v), v, .)) returns the
   219 . * smallest numeric value of v per pid, NOT the first-wave value; the
   220 . * correct form below marks the FIRST non-missing row per pid sorted by
   221 . * year, then propagates its value within pid.
   222 . capture program drop gen_vfirst
      
   223 . program define gen_vfirst
        1.     syntax , vname(varname) genname(name)
        2.     capture drop `genname'
        3.     tempvar seq mark tmp
        4.     bysort pid (year): gen `seq' = sum(!missing(`vname'))
        5.     by pid: gen `mark' = (!missing(`vname')) & (`seq' == 1)
        6.     gen `tmp' = `vname' if `mark'
        7.     by pid: egen `genname' = max(`tmp')
        8.     lab var `genname' "First-wave non-missing `vname'"
        9. end

   782 .     * ----------------------------------------------------------------

   784 .     *   Delta_never = Delta_base + sum_{v != v_base, v in V_supp} w_v * beta_
      > dev_v
   785 .     *                            + phi * (mu:never - mu:switcher_base)
   786 .     *   w_v = n^N_v / sum_{v' in V_supp} n^N_{v'}

   788 .     *  and beta_dev_{v_base} is 0 by the drop-a-dummy convention.)

      109.             }
      110.         }
