### Econometric proof style guide

Extracted 12 style patterns from a targeted read of Verdier (2020) main paper and appendix, saved to [docs/Econometric Proof Style.md](../Econometric Proof Style.md). Key patterns: preview the proposition in plain prose before stating it; restate what the proposition showed immediately after; keep the proposition statement itself trivially short (Verdier's Proposition 1 is literally "Under X and Y, as $n \to \infty$: [display formula]"); gloss each assumption sub-part individually with one "why it's here" sentence per part; say why each assumption is cheap to impose; use short modular hedges ("For simplicity, we assume..."); acknowledge scope in parentheticals rather than defensive clauses; open special-case sections with motivation + scope in two sentences. Also noted two anti-patterns: "Note that" is filler and should be cut; `\emph{}` emphasis should be avoided per project rules. The guide includes two side-by-side examples (proposition statement, ingredient preview) and a "when to deviate" caveat.
