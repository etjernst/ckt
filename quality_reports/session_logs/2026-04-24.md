## Decisions made

- **Q8 resolved (GMM weighting):** adopt `winitial(unadjusted, independent), onestep` for `run_grc_robust` and `run_grc_onestep`. Matches VV's `Table1/Code/robust.do`. Avoids the rank-deficient second-step weighting matrix issue. Originally flagged as blocking P3 only; P1 runs surfaced it as blocking P1 too.
- **Three-run reporting going forward:** each spec gets a `run_grc` (twostep, published baseline) + `run_grc_onestep` (apples-to-apples with robust) + `run_grc_robust` (VV). Keeps existing simple-spec tables unchanged while making the robust comparison airtight.
- **Degenerate-$v$ test design:** compare against `run_grc_onestep`, not `run_grc`, because both use onestep + `vce(cluster pid)` and collapse exactly when $|V|=1$. The `run_grc` comparison would fail because of different GMM weighting even though moment conditions are identical.
- **Naming convention (updates plan §P2):** `grcr_` prefix for robust, `grc1_` for onestep simple, to fit Stata's 32-char `_est_<name>` limit. Plan §P2 originally said `grc_robust_{country}_{spec}_{vindex}`, which is too long.
- **Smoke test staging:** chose `explorations/verdier/x_tza_robust_smoke.do` over editing `5_GrRC.do` + commenting out IDN/CHN. Keeps production code untouched during P1; P2 becomes additive.

## Substantive observations

- **Simple-spec twostep vs onestep barely moves $\hat\phi$** (TZA covs_all: $-0.72 \to -0.69$, within 1 SE). The two-step/onestep inconsistency across published-vs-new tables is small empirically, easing the paper's reporting burden.
- **Robust spec tightens the pro-poor pattern.** TZA $\hat\phi$ goes from $-0.69$ (onestep simple) to $-1.00$ (onestep robust, `vindex=region`). Allowing $\beta$ to vary by first-wave region absorbs across-region variation that was inflating the simple $\hat\phi$ toward zero.
- **$\hat\kappa$ identification is weak under onestep.** TZA robust SE on $\kappa$ is 683 (!!). The always-urban term in the GMM equation is only weakly identified in this specification. Matters for $\hat\Delta_{d_T}$ but not for $\hat\phi$ or $\hat\Delta_{d_N}$.

## Approaches rejected

- **Adding `nocons` inside the gmm substitutable expression `{beta_dev: varlist, nocons}`:** Stata rejects with `option nocons not allowed`. The bare `{eqname: varlist}` form already has no implicit constant in gmm, so this was redundant.
- **Keeping both specs on twostep:** rank-deficient weighting matrix caused non-convergence and singular SEs in the robust fit.
- **Changing `run_grc` to onestep throughout:** would re-estimate all published CKT simple-spec tables. User preference: keep `run_grc` as-is, add `run_grc_onestep` as a parallel sibling.
