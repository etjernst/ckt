## Decisions, with the why

The six design calls now encoded as MUST requirements in the spec:

- Shape: build logit (type-I EV) first, probit second. Both advisors independently landed here --- native McFadden micro-foundation under the i.i.d. $\nu$ assumption, closed-form, numerically cleaner at the never-migrant margin. Probit is a one-line swap.
- Anchor $\sigma_\eta$ to the observed rural-hukou never-migrant share (the moment the counterfactual actually perturbs), with the switcher share as an over-ID check. Theory advisor preferred the switcher share as the anchor; I took the econometrics view because the counterfactual moves the never-migrant left tail.
- Re-anchor $\sigma_\eta$ at every $(\phi,\beta)$ inference draw (econometrics advisor's call; anchor-once is internally inconsistent and is a robustness variant only).
- Headline regime-specific bases (internally consistent with the inversion CIs already estimated); common base is robustness, conditional on not breaking the within-regime $J$-test. Theory advisor wanted common base for a clean wedge normalization; deferred because re-estimating risks the $J$-test that justified the hukou split.
- $c=1$ (fully institutional wedge) as the maintained headline but flagged in prose as an upper bound, with the full $c\in[0,1]$ sweep as a sensitivity figure. $c$ is a separate reporting axis from $\sigma_\eta$.
- Report a $\phi^{rh}$-to-$\phi^{uh}$ slope range. The theory advisor's biggest flag: a $\beta$-only shift raises the migration level but does not mechanically restore slope-based sorting, so whether resorted workers carry the flat $\phi^{rh}$ or the steep $\phi^{uh}$ is a genuine modeling fork. Headline $\phi^{rh}$, bracket with $\phi^{uh}$.

Key structural insight that made the computation tractable: the GRC identifies trajectory-level objects ($\mu_d$, $\pi_d$), not individual $\theta_i$. Model each trajectory as a type with a single $\theta_d$; the i.i.d. $\nu$ shock with scale $\sigma_\eta$ supplies the within-type variation, so the resorting fraction is the logit choice probability $P_d(\beta) = \Lambda((\beta + \phi^{rh}\theta_d)/\sigma_\eta)$. Removing the barrier raises the intercept; the mass that flips times the consumption return is the gain. This is OQ1.
