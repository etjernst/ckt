## Why the monitor never fired

The error-and-completion monitor (most recently `bqb8zgv1c`) was watching for the success banner `display "overnight smoke complete"` at `_smoke_full.do:72`.
That banner never made it to the log.

Cause: `GRC_extras.do:126` ends with `exit, STATA clear`.
When called via `include` (from `_smoke_full.do:63`), that `exit` aborts Stata immediately.
The post-include lines in `_smoke_full.do` (`timer list`, the banner, the parent's own `exit, STATA clear`) never run.

This is a minor smoke-driver bug, not a results bug.
Two clean fixes:

1. Remove the `exit, STATA clear` from `GRC_extras.do`.
   Rationale: it should only live in scripts that are themselves the batch entry point, not in scripts that get `include`d.
2. Or move the banner / `timer list` above the `include` lines (clumsy; rejects).

Recommend fix 1 when convenient; not blocking.
