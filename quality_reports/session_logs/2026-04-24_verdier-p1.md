### Root cause (after reading VV's `Table1/Code/robust.do`)

My implementation adds $|V|-1$ cluster-choice interactions as free parameters. VV's implementation uses **village-demeaned** treatment indicators as **optimal instruments** while keeping only $\phi$ + covariate $\gamma$'s as parameters. Asymptotically equivalent; in finite sample with few clusters and rank-deficient cluster-robust weighting, the extra $|V|-1$ parameters make my GMM objective nearly flat in $\phi$. The derivation-memo §5 equivalence claim holds asymptotically but not in finite sample --- needs revision.

### Alpha-pooling derivation

Wrote [2026-04-24_alpha-pooling-derivation.md](file:///C:/git/ckt/docs/reviews/2026-04-24_alpha-pooling-derivation.md) working through the math. Key result:

Under trajectory pooling, the Verdier GMM moment for switcher $s$ implies
$$\Delta_{d_0, \text{fit}} = \bar\beta(s) + (\phi_{\text{true}} - \phi_{\text{fit}})(\mu_s - \mu_{d_0})$$
where $\bar\beta(s) = \sum_v \tilde w_{s,v} \beta(v)$, $\tilde w_{s, v} \propto n_{s,v} \cdot \text{Var}(D \mid s, v)$.

The estimator's bias is
$$\hat\phi - \phi = -\frac{\text{Cov}_s(\bar\beta(s), \mu_s - \mu_{d_0})}{\text{Var}_s(\mu_s - \mu_{d_0})}$$

**Headline finding:** the alpha-pooling problem affects the ESTIMATOR itself, not just the CI. The Option B CI inherits it but doesn't cause it.

Consistent under EITHER (a) $s$-invariant moment weights, OR (b) $\bar\beta(s)$ uncorrelated with $(\mu_s - \mu_{d_0})$ across switchers.
