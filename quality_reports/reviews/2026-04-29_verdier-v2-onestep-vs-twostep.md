## How to read this

- Pick onestep vs twostep per country. Working hypothesis: twostep when J is finite and
  the cell converged; otherwise onestep. SEs clustered at the village (vfirst) level.
- A blank J / J p means twostep did not converge (Stata cannot compute Hansen J without
  a valid weighting matrix at the optimum); fall back to onestep for that cell.
- Convergence column reports `e(converged)` for the main GMM call. `N` does NOT
  necessarily disqualify the cell --- check the .ster manually if you care.
