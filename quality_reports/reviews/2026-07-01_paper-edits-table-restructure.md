## What changed in the tables

- OLS tables lost their old column (1) (no covariates, no time fixed effects) and now have 6 columns.
  The Time FE indicator row now sits above the Covariates row.
  Old columns (2) through (7) are the new columns (1) through (6).
- GRC tables lost their old column (1) (the `c0` spec: no time fixed effects, no covariates) and now have 4 columns.
  Old columns (2) through (5) are the new columns (1) through (4).
- The main GRC tables now report a `95\% inv. CI` row under $\Delta_{\text{never}}$ and under $\bar\Delta$.
  These are the weak-identification-robust intervals from inverting the LCA overidentification test on a $\phi_0$ grid.
  $\Delta_{\text{always}}$ is not shown in the main tables.
