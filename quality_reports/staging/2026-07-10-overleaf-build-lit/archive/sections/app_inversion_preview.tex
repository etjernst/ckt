\section{Preview: LCA inversion confidence intervals}\label{app:inversion-preview}

This appendix is a temporary preview of the new GRC tables produced by the LCA inversion pipeline.
Each table now reports two extra rows underneath every $\Delta$ block and the $\phi$ block: a 90\% and a 95\% confidence interval obtained by inverting the LCA overidentification test on a $\phi_0$ grid.
A row entry of \texttt{empty} means no point in the grid satisfies the test at that level (typical for the no-covariate column~(1) where the LCA fits poorly without time fixed effects).
Multi-island intervals of the form $[-\infty, x] \cup [y, +\infty]$ reflect the singularity at $\phi = -1$ in the LCA mapping for $\Delta_{\text{always}}$ and are flagged in the postfoot note.

Tables \ref{tab:GRC_IDN_consumption_urban_unb_PREVIEW} and \ref{tab:GRC_TZA_consumption_urban_unb_PREVIEW} render the IDN and TZA cells from the unbalanced consumption-on-urban specification.
Estimates are unchanged from the canonical tables in Section~\ref{sec:grc-returns}; only the inversion CI rows and the new $\Delta_{\text{always}}$ block are added.
Once the rendering looks acceptable, this appendix will be deleted and the canonical \texttt{tables/GRC\_*\_consumption\_urban\_unb.tex} files refreshed in place.
