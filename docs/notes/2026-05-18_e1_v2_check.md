## What V2 does

V2 evaluates the E1 misallocation decomposition at the point estimate, using the inputs the production code will consume.
This is a code-consistency check, not a magnitude validation against an external benchmark.
The aggregate formula:

$$W_{\text{obs}} - W_{\text{zero}} = \sum_{\underline{d}} \pi_{\underline{d}} \cdot \Delta_{\underline{d}} \cdot \bar{D}_{\underline{d}}, \qquad W_{\text{opt}} - W_{\text{obs}} = \sum_{\underline{d}} \pi_{\underline{d}} \left[\max(0, \Delta_{\underline{d}}) - \Delta_{\underline{d}} \cdot \bar{D}_{\underline{d}}\right].$$

Trajectory categories used:

- $d_N$ (trajectory $= 1$): $\Delta = \hat\Delta_{d_N}$ from inversion.
- Balanced switchers (trajectory $\in 2 \ldots K-1$): $\Delta_{\underline{d}}$ from the unrestricted GRC `_d` ster.
- $d_T$ (trajectory $= K$): $\Delta = \hat\Delta_{d_T}$ from inversion.
- Lumped unbalanced switchers (trajectory missing, `unbalanced == 1`): $\Delta = $ `xb:unbalanced_choice` from the parent ster.

## Comparison to the 2026-05-13 back-of-envelope

The memo's BoE gave: $W_{\text{obs}} - W_{\text{zero}} \approx -0.04$ (IDN), $\approx -0.09$ (TZA); $W_{\text{opt}} - W_{\text{obs}} \approx 3.7\%$ (IDN), $\approx 22\%$ (TZA).

**TZA**: $W_{\text{obs}} - W_{\text{zero}}$ matches the BoE direction and magnitude ($-0.096$ production vs $-0.09$ memo).
The misallocation gap is larger than the BoE suggested ($+38.4\%$ vs $\approx 22\%$): the lumped switcher cell contributes $+0.069$ log pts and $d_N$ contributes $+0.111$ log pts, neither of which the BoE accounted for in detail.

**IDN**: the production number for $W_{\text{obs}} - W_{\text{zero}}$ flips sign relative to the BoE ($+0.046$ vs $-0.044$).
The flip is mechanical: in IDN's unbalanced spec, 89% of individuals sit in the lumped switcher cell with $\bar D \approx 0.48$ and $\Delta = $ `unb_choice` $= 0.116$, contributing $+0.050$ log pts that the BoE skipped.
The misallocation gap is also larger ($+6.4\%$ vs $\approx 3.7\%$), again driven by the lumped piece.

**V2 verdict: PASS on code consistency.**
The code reproduces the aggregate cleanly on its own inputs.
The BoE numbers from the memo were sketched with only $d_N$ and $d_T$ in mind and skipped the lumped switcher cell, which is large especially in IDN.

## Observations worth carrying forward

1. **The lumped-unbalanced cell is huge in IDN.**
   89% of IDN's unbalanced-spec individuals sit in this cell with non-trivial $\bar D \approx 0.48$ and $\Delta \approx +0.12$.
   For E1 to be defensible in IDN, the paper-side text needs to be transparent about how the lumped cell enters and what its $\Delta$ means.
   The current treatment uses `xb:unbalanced_choice` from the parent ster, which is the GMM's parameterization, but this deserves a one-sentence flag in the paper.

2. **TZA's $d_T$ piece dominates $W_{\text{obs}} - W_{\text{zero}}$ at $-0.139$ log pts.**
   Always-urban workers in TZA have $\hat\Delta_{d_T} = -0.70$ at the point estimate.
   Reading: observed migration has *negative* current welfare from the always-urban cell, consistent with the memo.

3. **TZA's $d_N$ piece dominates the misallocation gap at $+0.111$ log pts.**
   $\pi_{d_N} = 0.41$ and $\hat\Delta_{d_N} = +0.27$ deliver the bulk.
   This is the "non-migrants would gain from migrating" reading.
   The interpolation status (A6: TZA at 8% of switcher hull) means this magnitude rests on LCA holding near the lower edge of the support where it was fit.

4. **The lumped cell needs its own row in T1.**
   Currently the plan's T1 decomposition is $d_N$ / switchers / $d_T$.
   Under the unbalanced headline, the lumped-switcher cell deserves a fourth row.
