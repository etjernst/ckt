# Step 3: verdict

Severity: CLEAN WITH ONE SCOPE QUALIFIER.

All six substantive claims are supported.
The description must say that the paper combines its own US panel estimate with published Brazil and Indonesia estimates, or use the neutral phrase “panel evidence for the United States, Brazil, and Indonesia.”
It should not imply that Herrendorf and Schoellman independently estimate all three panels.

Recommended manuscript wording:

> Complementary work combines cross-country census evidence on sectoral wages and human capital with panel evidence for workers leaving agriculture in the United States, Brazil, and Indonesia.
> The switcher wage gains are positive but small relative to the sectoral wage gaps and are close to the barriers implied by the authors' selection model.
